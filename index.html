Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking your first consultation.

Is the dental treatment really completely painless?

Yes, absolutely. We utilize computerized local anesthesia (The Wand® STA), which delivers the numbing agent drop-by-drop below the pain threshold—meaning zero needle sting or pressure pain. We also offer nitrous oxide and conscious relaxation options for anxious patients.

How much do treatments cost, and are payment plans available?

We provide full financial transparency. Following your comprehensive 3D exam, you will receive an itemized, written quote with zero hidden charges. We offer 0% interest monthly installment plans over 6, 12, or 24 months, and our concierge team coordinates directly with major dental insurance providers.

How long do porcelain veneers and implants last?

Our custom E.max porcelain veneers routinely last 15 to 20+ years with proper oral hygiene. Our Swiss titanium dental implants carry a lifetime clinical warranty on the implant post and a 10-year warranty on the porcelain crown.

How quickly can I get an appointment for dental pain or emergency?

We reserve emergency priority slots every single day. If you are experiencing severe pain, a broken tooth, or facial trauma, contact our emergency hotline immediately at (555) 911-DENT or send a WhatsApp message for same-day evaluation.

What makes Your Clinic different from regular dental practices?

We integrate hospital-grade surgical sterility with cosmetic artistry and spa-grade relaxation. You receive individualized 60-minute unhurried consultations, 3D digital smile previews, ceiling entertainment, and direct access to board-certified specialists.

First Visit Guide

Preparing for your appointment

We strive to make your first visit smooth and relaxing. Here are a few simple tips to get the most out of your consultation:

  • Previous Records: If you have dental X-rays taken in the last 6 months, bring them or have your prior dentist email them.
  • Medications List: Please have a list of any current medications, supplements, or medical conditions ready.
  • Arrive 10 Minutes Early: Enjoy a fresh herbal tea or espresso in our acoustic lounge while completing your confidential digital health form.
  • Inspiration Photos: If you are interested in cosmetic veneers or smile design, bring photos of smiles you love!
